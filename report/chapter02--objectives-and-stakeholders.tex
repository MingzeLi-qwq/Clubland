\chapter{Objectives and stakeholders}

\section{Project objectives}
The primary objective of this project is to create a customized online
platform for students, allowing them to freely create or join clubs and
participate in club activities. The platform will provide an alternative
to mainstream social media platforms, enabling student organizations to
control their data, branding, and communications without the risk of
data exploitation or unnecessary commercialization.

The system aims to add value for various stakeholders in the following
ways: Clubs can track memberships, communicate with members, and
efficiently organize events. Clubs can create news, updates, and media
in a structured manner. Website administrators and club administrators
monitor and approve club pages to ensure compliance with institutional
policies. Clubs can design pages that reflect their identity and cater
to the needs of diverse groups, ranging from academic clubs to sports
teams.

By achieving these objectives, the platform enhances student community
engagement while maintaining privacy and institutional oversight.
\label{sect:objectives}

\section{Stakeholder}
The platform serves multiple stakeholder groups, including students,
general users, club managers, club members, and website administrators.

Stakeholders can be categorized into primary and secondary groups based
on their level of involvement and influence.
\label{sect:stakeholder}

\subsection{Stakeholder analysis}
\paragraph{Primary Stakeholders}\label{primary-stakeholders}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Student Clubs}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    The main users of the platform, responsible for creating and
    managing their club pages, events, and communications.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Club Managers}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    Responsible for approving, monitoring, and managing club pages.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{General Users}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    Can browse the website and club pages, and after registration, can
    apply to join clubs.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Club Members}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    End-users who join clubs, participate in events, and engage with
    content.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Secondary Stakeholders:}\label{secondary-stakeholders}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Developers and System Administrators}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    Responsible for maintaining and updating the platform, ensuring
    security and scalability.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Event Sponsors and Partners}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    External organizations that may support student clubs through
    sponsorships or event collaborations.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Alumni and External Members}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    Former students who wish to stay connected with clubs and
    participate in activities.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Key stakeholders}
Among all stakeholders, the following groups are critical to the
platform's success:

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Student Clubs}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    As the primary users, their engagement and satisfaction determine
    the platform's adoption and effectiveness.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Developers and System Administrators}:

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    They ensure the system remains functional, secure, and up-to-date.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

By addressing the needs and concerns of these key stakeholders, the
project aims to establish a sustainable and widely adopted system for
student clubs.
